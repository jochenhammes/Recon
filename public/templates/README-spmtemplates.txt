123I-FP-CIT SPECT and 18F-DOPA PET templates for SPM (García-Gómez et al.)

Files: spmtemplates_FPCIT_SPECT.nii.gz (source symFPCITtemplate_MNI_norm.nii), spmtemplates_FDOPA_PET.nii.gz
(source symFDOPAtemplate_MNI_norm.nii); 2 mm, 91 x 109 x 91, MNI152 (SPM), symmetrised.
Source: https://sourceforge.net/projects/spmtemplates/ (also NITRC "spmtemplates")

Terms of the authors: "These images are intended for free use by the neuroimaging community. However, we do ask
that you please cite the following reference in any publications which make use of these images":
  García-Gómez FJ, García-Solís D, Luis-Simón FJ, Marín-Oyaga VA, Carrillo F, Mir P, Vázquez-Albertino RJ.
  Elaboration of the SPM template for the standardization of SPECT images with 123I-Ioflupane.
  Rev Esp Med Nucl Imagen Mol 2013; 32(6):350-356.

Modification for Recon Studio: converted to little-endian float32 NIfTI-1 with gzip; values unchanged.
